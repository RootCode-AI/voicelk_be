# Code of Ethics

As contributors and maintainers of the VoiceLK project, we commit to the following ethical principles:

1. **Beneficence:** We strive to build software that benefits society and empowers users. We will consider the social impact of our work and aim to maximize positive outcomes.
2. **Non-maleficence:** We will avoid causing harm. We will design systems with security and privacy in mind, protecting user data and preventing misuse of the platform.
3. **Autonomy:** We respect the autonomy of our users. We will ensure transparency in how data is collected and used, giving users control over their information.
4. **Justice:** We promote fairness and equity. We will work to ensure our software is accessible to diverse populations and does not discriminate or reinforce biases.
5. **Integrity:** We will be honest and trustworthy. We will accurately represent the capabilities of our software and transparently communicate any limitations or known issues.
6. **Accountability:** We take responsibility for our work. We will be responsive to feedback, address concerns promptly, and continuously strive to improve the ethical standards of our project.
